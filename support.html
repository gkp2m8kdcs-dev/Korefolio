Frequently Asked Questions

Does Korefolio connect to a real broker?

No. Korefolio is a demonstration, education, and workflow-prototyping app. It does not connect to any broker, place real orders, or stream live market data. All charts, prices, and trades are generated locally and persisted on your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Korefolio does not require an account, login, or sign-up. Everything works fully offline, on-device, from the moment you launch the app.

How do I reset all my demo data?

Open the Profile tab and tap Reset Demo Data. This will clear all watchlists, portfolios, transactions, and attachments stored on your device. The action cannot be undone.

Why does the app ask for Camera or Photos permission?

Korefolio only requests these permissions when you tap Take Photo or Choose from Library inside the Add Transaction screen, so you can attach a receipt or note. Photos are stored locally in the app sandbox and never shared.

Can I export my data?

Currently Korefolio does not offer a built-in export. Because the app does not collect any data on a server, your data lives only on your device. As a workaround, you may screenshot any view of interest.

How is my data handled?

All data you create — watchlists, portfolios, transactions, photo attachments, and settings — is stored exclusively in the app sandbox on your device. No information is transmitted to any server, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are embedded. Please review our Privacy Policy for details.

Which markets are supported?

The demo universe spans global equities across the United States, Hong Kong, mainland China, Japan, the United Kingdom, and Germany, alongside a curated selection of major cryptocurrencies.

Why are the prices not changing in real time?

By design, Korefolio does not stream live market data. This keeps the app fully offline and private, and makes it appropriate for demonstration and educational use without any market-data licensing requirements.

Troubleshooting

The chart is not responding to gestures

Make sure you are using a single-finger pan to scroll and a two-finger pinch to zoom. A long press anywhere on the chart will activate the crosshair and reveal open / high / low / close values for that candle.

A transaction did not save

Confirm you tapped Save in the top-right corner of the Add Transaction screen. All required fields must be filled before save is enabled.

The watchlist is empty after reinstalling

Korefolio intentionally does not sync data to any server, so reinstalling the app resets watchlists, portfolios, and transactions to a fresh state.
