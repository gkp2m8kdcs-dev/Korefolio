Privacy Policy

Korefolio

Last updated: October 12, 2026  ·  Effective: October 12, 2026

Summary

Korefolio is built to be fully private. The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not transmit data to any server. Everything you create — watchlists, portfolios, transactions, and photo attachments — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Korefolio does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Account credentials — there is no login or sign-up
  • Location data, device identifiers, or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostic data
  • Billing information — the app has no purchases or subscriptions
  • Health, biometric, or sensitive personal data

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is generated and stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded to a server, shared with third parties, or transmitted off-device:

  • Watchlists — instruments you have added or removed from your curated list
  • Portfolios — name, color theme, and holdings composition
  • Transactions — buy/sell records, quantity, price, date, and notes
  • Photo attachments — receipts or notes you attach to a transaction, saved via the system Photos picker or captured via the device camera
  • App settings & preferences — chart type, time frame selection, and demo-data reset state

All of the above is persisted in the app's local sandbox and can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

3. Permissions Requested

Korefolio only requests the operating-system capabilities it strictly needs to operate, and only at the moment they are needed:

Camera

Used solely to capture a receipt or note photo to attach to a transaction. The camera is only accessed when you tap "Take Photo" within the Add Transaction screen. Photos you take are written to the app's local sandbox and never shared.

Photo Library

Used solely to select an existing photo to attach to a transaction. Korefolio uses the iOS Photos picker, which works on-device and does not require full library access.

Korefolio does not request microphone, contacts, location, motion, Face ID, or any other sensitive permission. There are no push notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

Korefolio contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no social login providers, no payment processors, and no remote data services. There is no browser or external link activity that would generate tracking data.

Market data, charts, and prices shown inside the app are generated and simulated locally. Korefolio does not connect to a broker, place real orders, or stream live market data.

5. Children

Korefolio does not target children under the age of 13. Because the app does not collect any personal information from any user, it is inherently compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regional regulations.

6. International Users

Because Korefolio does not transmit data off your device, there is no cross-border data transfer. Regardless of where you are located, the data you create stays on your device until you choose to delete it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides on your device, you control its retention. You may:

  • Use the in-app Reset Demo Data action in the Profile tab to clear all portfolios, transactions, and watchlists
  • Delete the app from your device to permanently remove all locally stored data

Deleting the app is the most thorough option. It removes every file in the app sandbox and cannot be undone, since no off-device backup is maintained.

8. Security

Local data is protected by iOS's standard app sandbox. Because nothing leaves the device, there is no network attack surface and no server-side breach risk. We recommend keeping your device OS up to date and using a device passcode or biometric lock.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above. Because the app does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you in advance by email; please review this page periodically.
